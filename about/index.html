The story

Why L180 Labs exists

Most AI companies sell you a tool and wish you luck. We saw that gap - between what AI promises and what businesses actually get - and decided to close it.

Our founder, Anirban, spent over 15 years building enterprise products for Fortune 100 organizations - designing systems used at scale across large workforces. The lesson from that decade and a half wasn’t about technology. It was about operations.

The companies that won weren’t the ones with the best software. They were the ones who rewired how their teams worked.

When AI reached a tipping point, the opportunity was clear: bring that same operational thinking to the businesses that needed it most - growing Indian businesses running on WhatsApp and willpower, and AI startups stretched thin on engineering bandwidth.

L180 Labs was built around a conviction: AI shouldn’t live in a slide deck. It should live inside your proposals, your follow-ups, your reports, your hiring pipeline - the workflows where your team spends its hours.

The “180” in our name isn’t about starting over. It’s about turning to face what’s next. Your business was built on instinct, grit, and hustle. We’re here to give it an operating engine that matches your ambition.
